## 2026-06-01 — map support for outdoor audio guides

- **Titles must describe, not tease.** "Outdoor audio guides without maps" confused the reader about intent. Titles should state the gist plainly — what was built, decided, or learned. Development notes are not op-eds.
- **No version numbers in prose.** Readers don't orient around version numbers. Establish the existing state first, then introduce what changed. "With the latest update..." works; "Version 1.0.1 adds..." does not.
- **These are development journey notes, not feature announcements.** The framing is: here is what I built, why, and what I ran into. First-person experience ("when I was putting together sample data...") is appropriate and preferred over neutral product description.
- **Future work: state the actual blocker, not just the intent.** Don't say "coming soon." Explain what's technically possible now, what hasn't been tested, and what the real gap is. Honesty about the pipeline problem (e.g. offline tiles requiring GIS expertise) builds more trust than a vague roadmap note.
- **Ramp-up before new information.** Don't open a section with the announcement. Establish the context (what already works, what the gap is), then introduce what changed. The reader needs a foothold first.
